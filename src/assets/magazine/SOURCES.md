# Image sources (magazine draft)

Every image here is public domain or CC0; each entry quotes the licence as the source states it. Files are renamed from the names in the heading.

### salk.jpg (1800×1418), was arch-modernist-salk.jpg
- **Title:** Salk Institute for Bological Studies, La Jolla, California *(the LoC title has this typo)*
- **Creator / institution:** Carol M. Highsmith, photographer. Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Carol M. Highsmith Archive (LC-HS503-1880, highsm 13139)
- **Source page:** https://www.loc.gov/item/2011631333/
- **Download URL:** https://tile.loc.gov/image-services/iiif/service:pnp:highsm:13100:13139/full/pct:50/0/default.jpg
- **License / rights statement (item record):** "No known restrictions on publication."
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. Bare concrete planes against a deep sky separate cleanly into two tones, and the receding blocks give a clear diagonal. Crop off the left third (sky and plaza) for a tall cover, or use it as a full-bleed horizontal with type in the sky.

### stairwell.jpg (1800×1200), was arch-stairs-stairwell.jpg
- **Title:** Looking down staircase. Federal Building and U.S. Courthouse, Dothan, Alabama
- **Creator / institution:** Carol M. Highsmith, photographer. Library of Congress, Carol M. Highsmith Archive (highsm 36548)
- **Source page:** https://www.loc.gov/item/2016645813/
- **Download URL:** https://tile.loc.gov/image-services/iiif/service:pnp:highsm:36500:36548/full/pct:50/0/default.jpg
- **License / rights statement (item record):** "No known restrictions on publication."
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. Glossy black railings against pale marble give near-binary contrast and nested rectangles. Crop to the upper two-thirds to drop the heavy black foreground railing. It also works rotated 90° as an abstract.

### window.jpg (1200×1800), was portrait-silhouette-window.jpg
- **Title:** Silhouette of a young woman in front of a window, Palais des Beaux-Arts, Lille, 2025
- **Creator / institution:** DimiTalen (own work), via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Silhouette_of_a_young_woman_in_front_of_a_window,_Palais_des_Beaux-Arts,_Lille,_2025.jpg
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f7/Silhouette_of_a_young_woman_in_front_of_a_window%2C_Palais_des_Beaux-Arts%2C_Lille%2C_2025.jpg
- **License (file page):** `{{self|cc-zero}}`: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. A tall arched window with a black figure silhouette reads as architecture and figure at once. The colorful facade seen through the glass flattens into mid-tones. The full vertical crop is a ready-made cover. *Caveat:* the view through the window shows a decorated facade opposite. It is incidental and goes abstract in duotone.

### vase.jpg (1351×1800), was object-vase-qingbai.jpg
- **Title:** Vase. China, Northern Song dynasty (960–1127), 11th–12th century. Porcelain with bluish white glaze (Jingdezhen qingbai ware). Accession 26.292.87
- **Creator / institution:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art (Gift of Mrs. Samuel T. Peters, 1926)
- **Source page:** https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/49914
- **Download URL:** https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/original/DP-15944-039.jpg
- **License:** The Met Collection API object record has `"isPublicDomain": true`, which marks it as Public Domain / Open Access under The Met's CC0 policy.
- **Two-tone note:** Excellent. A pale ovoid silhouette on a graded grey sweep gives a clean figure/ground split. The glaze crackle adds fine texture in the highlights. Centered vertical crop; trim the floor to taste.

### dunes.jpg (1800×1350), was texture-dunes-sossusvlei.jpg
- **Title:** Sossusvlei Dunes Namib (Namib desert dunes near Sossusvlei, Namib Naukluft National Park, Namibia), 2014-08-09
- **Creator / institution:** Vicartb (own work), via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sossusvlei_Dunes_Namib.jpg
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/Sossusvlei_Dunes_Namib.jpg
- **License (file page):** `{{self|cc-zero}}`: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. Knife-edge shadow ridges and a flat empty sky give a big clean field for type in the upper half. It reads as a landscape more than a texture. Crop to a wide panorama band through the dune ridges.

### fern.jpg (1146×1600), was Anna_Atkins_-_New_Zealand_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg
- **Title:** New Zealand. Anna Atkins, cyanotype, c. 1853–54 (Mia record). Accession 2006.10
- **Creator / institution:** Anna Atkins (English, 1799–1871). Minneapolis Institute of Art (The William Hood Dunwoody Fund), via Google Art Project / Wikimedia Commons
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anna_Atkins_-_New_Zealand_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg (museum record: https://collections.artsmia.org/art/99168)
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Anna_Atkins_-_New_Zealand_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg
- **License:** Commons file page: `{{PD-Art|PD-old-100-1923|deathyear=1871}}`. The Mia object record (https://search.artsmia.org/id/99168) says `"rights_type":"Public Domain"`.
- **Two-tone note:** Excellent. A single large fern frond in white sits on an even cyan ground, already a two-tone image. Map the blue to the dark stop and the frond to the light stop. *Caveat:* Atkins's handwritten caption "New Zealand" is printed in the plate, bottom centre. Crop it off (lose the bottom 10%) if the poster shouldn't carry it.

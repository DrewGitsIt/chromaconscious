# Image sources (trail app)

Every image here is public domain or CC0; each entry quotes the licence as the source states it. Files are renamed from the names in the heading. All were resized to a 1600px long edge and re-encoded as JPEG.

### forest.jpg (1067×1600), was Forest_path_disappearing_into_fog.jpg
- **Title:** Forest path disappearing into fog (Wiki Loves Forest in Armenia; categorised under Syunik Province), 2026-05-03
- **Creator / institution:** Mar Mkrtchyan (own work), via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forest_path_disappearing_into_fog.jpg
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/92/Forest_path_disappearing_into_fog.jpg
- **License (file page):** `{{self|cc-zero}}`: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. Dark trunks against a flat white fog give a clean figure/ground split, and the fog is a ready-made light field for type. The path is a faint dark strip at the lower left, and the green ground cover becomes a dark band along the bottom. Use the full vertical frame on a phone, or crop to the top two-thirds for trees only.

### ridge.jpg (1600×916), was Eagletail_Mountains_Wilderness_(9412575311).jpg
- **Title:** Eagletail Mountains Wilderness (Arizona)
- **Creator / institution:** Bureau of Land Management, via Wikimedia Commons (Flickr upload)
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eagletail_Mountains_Wilderness_(9412575311).jpg
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Eagletail_Mountains_Wilderness_%289412575311%29.jpg
- **License (file page):** `{{PD-USGov-BLM}}`: work of the Bureau of Land Management, US federal government, public domain
- **Two-tone note:** Excellent. Stacked ridgelines fade from near-black in front to pale haze behind, so a gradient map gives clean steps of tone. The jagged peaks in the middle are a clear focal point, and the cloudy sky at the top is free for type. Crop to a square around the peaks for a card, or keep it wide as a header.

### lake.jpg (1600×1061), was Alpine_Lakes_Wilderness,_Okanogan-Wenatchee_National_Forest_(37171631555).jpg
- **Title:** Alpine Lakes Wilderness, Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest
- **Creator / institution:** U.S. Forest Service – Pacific Northwest Region, via Wikimedia Commons (Flickr upload)
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alpine_Lakes_Wilderness,_Okanogan-Wenatchee_National_Forest_(37171631555).jpg
- **Download URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/92/Alpine_Lakes_Wilderness%2C_Okanogan-Wenatchee_National_Forest_%2837171631555%29.jpg
- **License (file page):** `{{PD-USGov-USDA-FS}}`: work of the US Forest Service, public domain
- **Two-tone note:** Strong. Rocky peaks and a small glacier are mirrored in a still lake with a dark shoreline of conifers. The black tree masses and pale snow and sky give a wide tonal range. The symmetry suits a centred crop. *Caveat:* a tiny yellow tent sits on the far shore, centre right (about 1060, 540). It disappears in duotone but could be cropped out.

- **Creator / institution:** U.S. Forest Service – Pacific Northwest Region, via Wikimedia Commons (Flickr upload)

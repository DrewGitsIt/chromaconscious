Build the home screen for **Sunshare**, a community-solar co-op's mobile web app. Members share a neighborhood solar array and get credits on their power bill.

The screen is viewed on a phone (390px wide). It should show:

- A greeting and this month's **solar credit earned**, in dollars. This is the number members care most about.
- **This month's electricity use against the member's budget**: 312 kWh used of a 400 kWh budget, with 9 days left in the month.
- **Today's output** from the shared array: 1,840 kWh, 12% above forecast.
- A heads-up that **peak pricing runs 5–8 pm today**, and a suggested action: "Shift laundry to off-peak".
- **Recent activity**: your May bill was paid, a credit was applied, and you invited a neighbor (pending).
- A bottom tab bar: Home, Usage, Array, Account.

The brand colors are already chosen. Use the ChromaConscious theme below; its variables are in `theme.css`. Make it look like a polished, modern product that people are glad to open.

1 · What a “degree of hue” is

OKLCH hue is an angle on a perceptual color wheel. Every swatch here has identical lightness (0.65) and chroma (0.14) — only the hue angle changes, in 15° steps. Two swatches apart ≈ 30°: roughly the distance between neighboring nameable color families.

3 · The accent rule: 15°–60° from primary

Scoring an accent against an olive primary (H=123°, leftmost). Under ~15° away it reads as a variation of primary, not an accent — hue-fit 0. The score ramps up between 15° and 60°, then stays maxed: anything 60°+ away is fully “a different color”.

5 · The “designer window” for primary lightness

Same blue at different lightness. Scoring prefers mid-tones (gaussian centered L=0.55). Separately, fidelityAdjust clamps the seed into L 0.45–0.68 at fidelity 0 and releases it toward the input as fidelity → 1. Mid-tones are where a color can be a button fill, carry white or black text, and survive both modes.

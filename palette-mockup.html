you were right — and it changes the rule

Round 1 deleted the role badge along with the grip and the ✕. That was one deletion too many: the badge looked like chrome but it was the only door to pinning. Here's the line I should have drawn:

Delete widgets that duplicate information. Never delete the only verb. Where information must be displayed anyway, make that the control — it costs zero new chrome.

By that rule the grip and the ✕ still go (the color already affords dragging; kill can hide until hover). But the role has to be shown regardless — so making the role label itself the button is free.

grip dotsgone — pure chrome, the color is the handle
inline ✕gone — hover-revealed, destructive shouldn't hold real estate
separate swatchgone — there's just the color
badge chromegone — pill background/border fought the color
the role verbrestored — as the label itself, not a widget beside it

what changed in each

1 · chips. The role became a pill-shaped hit area drawn from the color — on hover it fills with a well of the chip's own contrast ink at low alpha, so the affordance is still zero foreign chrome. The kill's 22px is now permanently reserved rather than slid into on hover: round 1 had the role sliding left to clear room, which would have moved a click target out from under the pointer. Nothing moves now.

2 · tiles. The 9px role caption was dead text; it's now a full-width pill under the tile — the tile's primary verb, and a far bigger hit target than the old badge ever was. Tiles widened to 74px to carry real role names.

tryClick any role. Then pin one color to a role another color already holds — the displaced chip pulses red and drops to unused, which is the seat transfer made visible.

the popover

Same information architecture the shipped menu has, given room to breathe: the color and its current casting, then the why-lines from explain.ts, then all seven targets (six roles + chart).

  • Each target shows who holds it now as a small swatch, with a hatched placeholder when the engine is inventing that role. You can see what you'd displace before you click.
  • Consequence subtitles use pinConsequence's real phrasing — benches #316264, takes a seat the engine was inventing, frees primary for #79f77a.
  • Unpin is a footer row, present only when pinned.
  • It's portaled to the panel, not the scrolling body — which solves the clipping snag I flagged in the last sheet rather than just noting it again.

three targets, and why that's still fewer

Each color now has three: the color area (picker), the role (pin menu), the hover ✕ (remove). The old row had five, two of which were pure furniture. Three distinct verbs on three spatially distinct areas is the floor — you can't go lower without hiding a capability behind a right-click or a mode.

open

  • Chart is a pooled seat — several colors can hold it at once, so its "who holds it" swatch shows the first of the set. Should it show a cluster of dots instead?
  • In tiles, an 8-color palette makes 8 role pills — a lot of repeated uppercase. Worth showing the role only on hover + when pinned?
  • Should dragging a chip onto a role be a second path to pinning? Discoverable, but it competes with drag-to-reorder.
  • Auto-contrast ink is still a luminance threshold; the real one should call the engine's APCA solver.
